    Looking for your Micro Plus

    Turn on the amp and close Fender Tone on your other devices. This page connects by itself once the amp is found.

    Preset editor

    Select a pedal or the amp to adjust it. Changes reach the amp as you make them, about once a second, and aren't kept until you save.
